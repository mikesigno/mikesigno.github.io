Terms

How to book a repair, my rates, and the terms that apply to every job.

Last updated: November 2026

By booking a repair you agree to these terms.

Rates

I charge £50 per hour + VAT for all repairs, with a minimum charge of £95 + VAT per item. On location the minimum charge is 4 hours, plus travel time and costs.

Estimates and quotes

I work on old, often obsolete equipment with discontinued parts, so time and cost can vary. I need to find the fault before I can give an accurate estimate. For major repairs or restorations (close to the value of the item), I'll quote the time and price before starting.

Updates and approval

I'll keep you updated as the repair goes on. If the plan changes, for example because parts are expensive or the bill would make the repair not worthwhile, I'll ask for your approval before going ahead. Complex repairs take time, so thank you for your patience.

What I take on

I don't repair kits or self-assembled gear, so please tell me if that's what you have. On synths and samplers I only do minor work, such as a voice chip change or a power supply repair.

On location

Most work is done at my workshop at Baltic Studios. When I travel to you, travel time and costs are added and the minimum charge is 4 hours.

Courier and collection

If you send gear by courier, you arrange both drop-off and collection. Equipment not collected within 30 days of being ready may be subject to storage fees.

Unfamiliar equipment

I don't charge for time spent learning a model that's new to me, only the hours the job would take if I already knew it. In busy periods I may have to turn down unusual or lower value items.

Vintage and consumer equipment

Very old gear (60 to 70 years or more) and some consumer equipment carry extra risk. Plastics break, connectors fail, and some units were never meant to be taken apart. Unrelated faults can also appear during a service. I'll always talk you through the risks, but I can't absorb all the extra time, and the cost may sometimes exceed the value of the item.

Microphone capsule cleaning

Typically £100 to £150, depending on the microphone and its condition. I only recommend it when the capsule is clearly affecting the sound, as it can only be done a limited number of times before a reskin is needed. Old capsules are fragile, so I can't take responsibility if a diaphragm fails or detaches during or after cleaning.

Liability

Because of the age and nature of this equipment, servicing carries some risk. By booking, you accept that:

  • equipment may have pre-existing faults
  • parts may fail during testing or repair
  • some faults may get worse in the process
  • replacement parts may be scarce or cost extra

Payment and changes

Payment is by bank transfer. Prices and these terms may change over time. By booking, you agree to the terms current at the time of booking.

Get in touch

Got something that needs looking at?

Outboard, microphones, consoles or tape machines. Tell me what it is and what it is doing, and I will get back to you.
