SL613E test jig

Tests every function of a single SSL channel, with no console needed.

Enquire →

About

Based on the original SSL 613 test jig, along with its extremely rare SL354 Ultimation add-on, the SL613E powers up a single channel away from the console and lets you test every function, so channels can be fixed without shutting the room down.

Supplied with a channel extender, the jig powers the module and simulates every input and output the channel sees in the console, both audio and logic, so the whole channel can be tested on the bench.

It works with every SSL 4000 and 6000 channel type: E Series, G Series (VCA and Ultimation), dual line and stereo modules. Every jig comes aligned and ready to use.

It is made for techs who don't have time to build their own, and for studios that can't afford console downtime. I use it myself across studios in London: people send me channels and I send them back fixed.

Built in a modular way across three PCBs, it is easy to service, with high quality Grayhill switches, a linear power supply and a front panel that replicates the original.

Specifications

  • Every SSL 4000 and 6000 channel: E Series (VCA), G Series (VCA and Ultimation), 611 Dual Line and 611S stereo
  • Module select with colour-coded panel legends for each module type
  • Status select: Rec + M/R, Rec, Rep, Mix, Mix + Rec
  • Input select: mic, line, inserts, mix amp, slate and monitor
  • Output select over five cascaded knobs: input, mic, insert, group, meter feed, main mix buses, stereo cue, auxes 1 to 4, AFL and all 32 routing buses
  • VCA and Ultimation fader simulation (−50 to +10 dB), with VCA bypass for exact unity
  • Centre section: VCA group mute, PFL, master input flip, master ready group, slate, fader flip, Total Recall, fader select
  • Bus lines with LEDs: monitor cut, channel cut, solo disable, dynamics link
  • Status LEDs: VCA, tape, group, record and record contact; start and stop sense on the 611S
  • Audio in and out, plus a DC-coupled BNC meter output for reading the VCA control voltage
  • Channel extender included, with a mic mute switch for checking the noise floor
  • Linear power supply with +18, +13 and −18 V rail LEDs
  • Modular build on three PCBs, Grayhill switches, front panel replicating the original
  • Supplied aligned and ready to use
