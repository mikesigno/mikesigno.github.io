About

The jig powers up a channel independently from the console, so every input and output can be tested quickly. It is compatible with Neve V Series (VR) consoles and the 88R.

Each jig comes with an extender, a PDF manual and VR servicing guidance, a linear power supply and quality switches. Measurements are made with your own test equipment.

Each one is built to order. Lead time is currently two to three months.

Specifications

  • Neve V Series (VR) and 88R
  • Powers a single channel away from the console
  • Extender included
  • PDF manual and VR servicing guidance
  • Linear power supply
  • Quality switches throughout
  • Built to order, two to three months
